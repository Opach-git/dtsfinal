Saving the enviroment one device at a time

At Kiwi Repair, we realise that the soloution to protecting the enviroment is not just removing waste, but making sure that it never gets there in the first place. We take in all kinds of electronic devices and repair them to ensure they don't become e-waste and they can have a longer life with the consumers.

What we do

At Kiwi Repair, we help give damaged and unwanted electronics a second life. Instead of throwing away a device when it stops working, we assess the problem and look for ways to repair and restore it.

Repairs

We repair a range of electronic devices, including phones, laptops, tablets and other common household electronics.

Reuse & Restoration

Whenever possible, we restore devices so they can continue being used rather than becoming e-waste. This helps reduce the demand for new electronics and the resources needed to produce them.

Responsible E-Waste

When a device cannot be repaired, we make sure it is handled responsibly. Parts and materials that can be recovered are separated and recycled where possible.


Our aim is simple: keep electronics in use for longer and reduce the amount of e-waste produced.
