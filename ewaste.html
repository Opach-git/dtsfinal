Key information on E-Waste

New Zealand generates around 100,000 tonnes of e-waste annually, but only about 2% is recycled while the rest goes to landfills.

What is E-Waste?

  • Any unwanted or end-of-life device requiring a plug, cord, or battery to operate.

  • Includes computers, phones, TVs, appliances, and loose batteries.

  • Contains toxic materials like lead, mercury, cadmium, and lithium, which can spark fires in rubbish trucks or leach into soil and water
