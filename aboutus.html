About us

Kiwi Repair was founded with a simple idea: not every broken electronic device needs to become waste. Every year, thousands of electronic devices are thrown away because they are damaged, outdated, or simply not working properly. Many of these devices could still have years of useful life left in them with the right repairs. We wanted to create a solution that focused on preventing e-waste before it happens. Starting as a small local repair project, Kiwi Repair has grown into a service dedicated to giving electronics a second chance. We accept a wide range of devices, from phones and laptops to other household electronics, and work to repair and restore them wherever possible. Our goal is not just to fix electronics. We want to encourage people to repair, reuse and look after the devices they already own instead of immediately replacing them. By extending the life of a device, we can reduce the amount of electronic waste entering landfills and reduce the need for new resources to be used to produce replacements. At Kiwi Repair, we believe that small changes can make a big difference. A repaired device is one less device becoming e-waste, and one more device that can continue to be useful.
